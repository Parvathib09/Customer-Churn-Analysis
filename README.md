# Customer-Churn-Analysis

Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard built using Power BI to analysis customer behaviour and identify churn patterns.

Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

### Project Overview

The Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to help businesses understand customer behavior and identify the factors contributing to customer churn. By transforming raw customer information into meaningful visual insights, the dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor churn trends, evaluate customer segments, and make informed business decisions aimed at improving customer retention.

This project demonstrates the use of Power BI for business intelligence by combining data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations into a user-friendly dashboard.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze customer churn patterns and trends.
- Identify the factors that influence customer retention.
- Compare churn across different customer segments.
- Monitor customer distribution based on tenure, contract type, and services.
- Enable interactive exploration through filters and slicers.
- Support data-driven decision-making for customer retention strategies.

---

### Tools & Technologies

---

### Dashboard Features

- KPI cards displaying Total Customers, Churned Customers, and Average Tenure.
- Overall Customer Churn Distribution.
- Churn Analysis by Contract Type.
- Churn Analysis by Internet Service.
- Churn Analysis by Payment Method.
- Customer Distribution by Tenure.
- Senior Citizen Churn Analysis.
- Monthly Charges vs. Churn Comparison.
- Customer Details Table with interactive filtering.
- Dynamic slicers for detailed analysis by Gender, Partner, and Dependents.

---

### Key Insights

- Customers with Month-to-Month contracts show the highest churn rate, making contract type a major factor in customer retention.
- Fiber Optic customers experience higher churn compared to customers using DSL services.
- Customers with One-Year and Two-Year contracts are significantly more likely to remain with the company.
- Higher Monthly Charges are associated with an increased likelihood of customer churn.
- Customers with longer tenure demonstrate stronger loyalty and lower churn rates.
- Interactive filtering helps identify high-risk customer groups and supports targeted retention strategies.
